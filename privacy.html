Ganesh Music – Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Ganesh Music is a personal music player app developed by Konne Ganesh. This notice explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

The app does not run its own servers and does not collect, store or sell personal information about you. Your favorites, playlists and listening history are saved only on your own device.

Third-party services

To find and play music, the app connects directly from your device to these services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Spotify (login and song search; playback happens in the Spotify app)
  • YouTube (search and the official embedded player)
  • Audius and Jamendo (music search and streaming)
  • LRCLIB (song lyrics)

When you log in to a service, you sign in on that service's own page. The app never sees your password. Access tokens are kept only while the app is in use and are not sent anywhere else.

Device permissions

The app asks for access to audio files on your device so it can play your local music, and for internet access so it can stream online music. Local files are not uploaded anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
